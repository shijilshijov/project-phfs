Prepare a video for Instagram's Ray-Ban Meta / Spin View detection.

✅ Proper Meta-style conversion

Video recognition is not achieved by simply resizing to 1440×1920. Instagram checks QuickTime/MOV structure and metadata. The converter below uses a dedicated browser implementation for this purpose.

Open Ray-Ban Meta Converter
📱 iPhone steps
  1. Tap Open Ray-Ban Meta Converter.
  2. Choose your video from Photos/Files.
  3. Use Video mode and choose 3:4.
  4. Convert and download the resulting file.
  5. Save the downloaded file to Photos.
  6. Open Instagram and upload the saved video.
⚠️ Important
Do not send the converted file through WhatsApp, Telegram, or another editor before Instagram. Apps that rewrite the MOV container can remove the QuickTime structure that Instagram uses for the Meta glasses badge.

Why this change: a normal H.264 MP4 with 1440×1920 dimensions is not enough. Independent testing reports that the relevant video marker is in a QuickTime meta atom directly under moov, with specific QuickTime keys and track structure. cite cannot be embedded here; see the project documentation linked from the converter if you want the technical details.
